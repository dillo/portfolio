## Overall Impression

The core "Field Notebook" identity is real, consistent, and well-executed at the component level — this is not a templated AI-portfolio look. The gap is that the site's two highest-stakes moments (the résumé, and the point right after a visitor decides to reach out) sit outside that identity entirely, and one documented product constraint (LinkedIn/Medium-only contact) is quietly violated twice — once in the footer, once inside the résumé PDF's own header. Neither is a hard visual defect; both are trust/consistency problems at exactly the moments that matter most for a job-search site.

## What's Working

1. **Design-system fidelity**: every card, border, radius, and translucency value checked against `DESIGN.md` matched exactly — an unusually tight link between the written spec and the shipped Tailwind classes.
2. **Bespoke, accessible diagrams**: `components/guides/diagrams/*` are hand-built and lesson-specific rather than stock or generic, and carry real accessibility markup (`role="img"`, `aria-label`, `<title>`) that's easy to skip and wasn't.
3. **Outcome-specific voice**: real org names and concrete numbers throughout, instead of generic self-description — this is what actually differentiates the copy from a template, and both assessments independently confirmed it.

## Priority Issues

**[P0] Footer GitHub link contradicts the documented contact-channel constraint, on every page.**
Why it matters: `PRODUCT.md` states contact is LinkedIn and Medium *only*, explicitly "a deliberate choice, not a gap." `components/Footer.tsx` renders a GitHub icon linking to `https://github.com/dillo` on every single page anyway, and the URL itself reads unverified.
Fix: remove the GitHub link, or get explicit product sign-off on adding GitHub as a contact channel and verify the URL before restoring it.
Suggested command: `/impeccable harden` (Footer.tsx) — or just a direct manual edit, this doesn't need a design pass.

**[P0] The résumé PDF itself exposes an email address, contradicting the site's core contact constraint.**
Why it matters: the embedded/downloadable `public/resume.pdf` shows `dillo.raju.media@proton.me` in its header — the exact channel `PRODUCT.md` says is never exposed — and `/resume` is explicitly called out as "the canonical, most-trusted artifact." This isn't a UI bug; it's a content decision in an asset outside Impeccable's reach, but it's the single most visible violation of the stated constraint.
Fix: this is yours to resolve directly (edit the PDF, or explicitly revise the "no email" constraint in `PRODUCT.md` to scope it to the website only). No design command touches PDF content.

**[P1] Résumé page breaks the visual system at the highest-intent page, and duplicates none of its content in the site's own type.**
Why it matters: verified live — in dark mode, the page header goes dark correctly but the embedded PDF stays a stark white native-viewer panel, the single largest visual discontinuity on the site, at the page `PRODUCT.md` calls the primary conversion artifact. A fast-scanning recruiter (the site's actual named persona) who doesn't zoom/scroll the embed never sees the strongest proof points in the notebook's own typography.
Fix: add a short on-page summary (3-4 headline achievements) in notebook type above or beside the PDF embed, so the résumé's key evidence doesn't live solely inside an unstyled iframe.
Suggested command: `/impeccable layout` (or `/impeccable typeset`) on `app/resume/page.tsx`.

**[P1] No custom error page; Guides routes hard-fail on any stale or mistyped slug.**
Why it matters: confirmed live — no `app/not-found.tsx` or `app/error.tsx` exists, and `dynamicParams = false` on both Guides route types means a bad `/guides/[series]/[lesson]` URL renders Next.js's bare default 404 with zero way back into the site beyond the persistent nav. With 22 lesson slugs already live and more planned (Guides is a confirmed ongoing commitment per `PRODUCT.md`), this will get hit.
Fix: add a notebook-styled `not-found.tsx` with links back to `/guides` and `/`.
Suggested command: `/impeccable harden`.

**[P1] No visible focus-state styling anywhere in the codebase.**
Why it matters: a grep across the codebase confirms zero `focus:`/custom outline rules; keyboard users fall back entirely to the browser default focus ring, which reads thin and low-contrast against the dark theme. This is the single clearest accessibility gap found.
Fix: add `focus-visible:` treatments (ring or border shift in the terracotta accent) to interactive elements — nav links, buttons, cards that are links.
Suggested command: `/impeccable audit` (to confirm scope precisely) then `/impeccable harden`.

**[P2] Guides doesn't visually distinguish itself from the marketing pages, despite being pitched as an equal, ongoing pillar.**
Why it matters: `lib/guides/ai-engineering/index.ts` sets the series accent to `accent` (terracotta) rather than `signal` (teal) — the second "ink" the system defines never surfaces above the individual-callout level, so `/guides` and `/guides/ai-engineering` are chromatically identical to Home/About/Contact until a reader is several clicks deep inside one lesson's "Tip" callout.
Fix: give the Guides section itself (index card, series header) the teal accent treatment already defined for it, so the section reads as visually distinct at the level a fast scanner would notice.
Suggested command: `/impeccable colorize` or `/impeccable layout` on `app/guides/page.tsx` and `app/guides/[series]/page.tsx`.

**[P2] About's Toolbox groups exceed comfortable chunking, and the nav's "AI / modernization" label sits under the accepted minimum text size.**
Why it matters: the "Architecture & Delivery" toolbox group renders 10 chips flat with no sub-prioritization — well past the ≤4-item chunking guideline for a single visual group. Independently, the browser overlay confirmed a 10px nav sub-label (`components/Nav.tsx:31`) sitting under the site's own 11px floor on every page.
Fix: split dense toolbox groups into "core" vs. "also familiar with," or accept the density as a deliberate reference-list exception; bump the nav sub-label to 11px minimum.
Suggested command: `/impeccable layout` (toolbox) + a one-line fix in `Nav.tsx` (font size, no command needed).

**[P3] Heavy em-dash usage in Guides prose reads as an AI-writing tell.**
Why it matters: both assessments independently flagged this — Assessment B counted 19 em-dashes in one lesson body; Assessment A separately noted the same pattern across multiple lessons before seeing the detector output. Two independent, isolated reads agreeing is a strong signal, and it works against the "field notebook, written by a person" voice the system is going for.
Fix: vary sentence construction — commas, periods, or parenthetical asides — in place of some em-dashes.
Suggested command: `/impeccable clarify` on the Guides lesson content.
